Silent film. Every beat is real captured product footage from
`assets/footage/` (1080x1080 @ 2x, recorded on the live app); the only
authored layer is kinetic type in the product's own palette and type
(plano paper, ink navy, Fira Sans / Fira Sans Condensed / Fira Code).
On-screen lines sit in the lower third over the footage, never covering
the map's center. No voiceover: the written lines below are the on-screen
captions; their reveals pace the shots.

## Video direction

Pacing: held beats over spectacle — the film breathes the way the product
breathes (boot hold, terminus hold). Cuts are hard cuts; the only
transitions are the ones the product itself owns (day→night fold, camera
dives). Type: sentence-case display in Fira Sans, tracked-caps mono
micro-labels for meta beats, all on the cobalt-grid frame remixed onto
traza's palette (paper #EDF0F5, ink #1C2733, navy #0F4E9E). Nothing
teleports: every line morphs or slides the way the LED board does.
Lower-third type never covers the map's middle band.
